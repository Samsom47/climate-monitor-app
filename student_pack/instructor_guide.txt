SpacePoint Climate Monitoring – Instructor Guide
================================================

Learning Objectives
-------------------
- Understand basic land-cover classification from aerial images
- Calculate and interpret vegetation cover (green pixel ratio)
- Explain the urban heat island effect
- Read simple environmental sensor data
- Link observations to real climate actions

Suggested Time: 45–60 minutes
Materials: Laptop or mobile phone + the SpacePoint website

How to Run the Session
----------------------
1. Open the website (streamlit run app.py or shared link)
2. Let students explore for 10 minutes
3. Ask them to complete the worksheet
4. Discuss answers as a group

Answer Guidance
---------------
1. Students should describe vegetation, water, buildings, soil, etc.
2. Hotter zones = bright/red areas in the heatmap (urban/concrete)
3. Higher green % = more vegetation
4. Possible problems: heat islands, low greenery, moderate/poor air quality
5. Possible actions: plant trees, create parks, reduce vehicle emissions, green roofs

Discussion Questions
--------------------
- Why do cities get hotter than the countryside?
- How can drones help us understand climate issues?
- What simple actions can students take in their own area?

Extension Ideas
---------------
- Students bring their own phone photos of parks vs streets
- Compare green ratio of different local areas
- Discuss real news about heatwaves or air quality
